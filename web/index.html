Why robots fall

Robots doing complex tasks fail on the edge cases.

Physical Intelligence says it plainly: robots "can only operate in environments they've been trained in."

Robot data trails the data behind language models by an estimated 100,000 years, so labs now pay people by the hour to move in front of cameras. Almost none of it records the ground underfoot, so we tie every motion to the terrain it happened on and train robots on that same ground.
